Draw · Scheme · Win

A party drawing game where secret bonus items can steal the round — draw on paper, score on your phone.

How to Play

Read once, play forever

1
Get paper & pens
Everyone grabs something to draw on — paper, a whiteboard, a napkin. No tablets needed.
2
See your scene & secret items
Everyone sees the same scene prompt. Then the app reveals 3–4 secret bonus items — sneak them into your drawing for extra points.
3
Draw! Timer is live
Pick your mode — 30 sec chaos, 60 sec standard, or 2 min for detail. The ring counts down. Pencils down when it hits zero.
4
Hold up your drawings & score
Everyone reveals. Tap which bonus items each player included, rate creativity with stars, and vote for the best overall drawing.
🏅 How points work
+1–4per bonus item drawn
+1–3creativity stars
+3best drawing vote
💡 Pro tip
The funnier (or worse) the drawing, the better. Bad art wins hearts. Bonus items hidden in clever spots score big.
